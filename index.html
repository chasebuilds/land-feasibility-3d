Layers

Lots are squares of the minimum size on a grid, turned to whichever angle fits the most lots. A pad goes in only where at least 90% of it is under 15% slope. Each pad is set at the average ground under it, so cut about equals fill. Road frontage: every lot is assumed to front a new internal road. The road is not drawn, graded or costed here beyond the site work per lot.

Every dollar figure here is an illustrative assumption. Replace each one with real quotes and comps.
